# Car differentiation in the v0 prototype?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: (none)

Is "different cars feel different" (wheel size, suspension) validated in v0
or deferred?

## Answer

Resolved 2026-10-05: cars must be **adjustable at runtime in the prototype**
— tuning the feel is the point. Saving adjusted cars as presets follows
naturally; three starting presets are welcome as long as adjusting is easy.

- Start with 1–3 cars; visuals stay simple, but parameters must be visible
  (e.g. a bigger wheel looks bigger).
- Deliberate no-YAGNI: done well, unique or even licensed real cars (e.g.
  Majorette, cited as the best brand for the game) could become a thing.
